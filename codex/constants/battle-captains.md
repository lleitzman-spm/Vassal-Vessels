A captain rides with one of his units, which is the price of the heart he puts into the men around him. If that unit breaks he may fall with it; if it is destroyed he does. His contingent then loses its wits: no orders heeded at all for half a minute, none of his own-hand actions, and then a successor who is three-quarters the man at everything. Killing the enemy's best captain can decide a day, and so can gambling with yours.
