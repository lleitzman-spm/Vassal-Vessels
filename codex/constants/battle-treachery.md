How the battle spends the court's treachery number — the contract leaves the how to the battle, so the battle must say. Every two seconds, a contingent whose treachery is 40 or more, on a side whose Tide has sunk to -10 or worse, may act: at 60+ it DEFECTS to the enemy; at 40-59 it withdraws from the field. The chance per check is (treachery - 30) x 2 per mille.
