# Battle: Melee

> **STANDING — BUILT**  
> Implemented in code and checkable against the tree — a module or a test stands under it.  
> *Derived from the tree — the code is there to be read.*

Every tick, the men who can actually reach each other take a swing. Whether it lands is 'hit'; whether it does anything through armour is 'wound'. Reach decides the first six seconds. Then the press: long weapons drop to one fighting rank, take their crowd penalty, and the reach advantage becomes half of itself as a PENALTY — computed from primary weapons even after sidearms are drawn, because the man is still tangled with his pike. Short weapons gain +12 penetration in the press, because at grappling distance you aim at the gaps in the armour. Backing out of a melee lets the enemy hit you two and a half times as hard for one tick.
