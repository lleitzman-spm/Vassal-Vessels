# Battle: Missiles

> **STANDING — BUILT**

> *Derived from the tree — the code is there to be read.*

Arrows are shot at a patch of ground, they FLY — three seconds to reach 150 metres — and land on whoever is standing there; a target that moved is missed, and the sim never leads the target. How many hit depends almost entirely on how tightly packed the target stands. Arrows kill slowly and frighten badly (the 1400 fear multiplier): their real job is to make the enemy breakable, and then you charge.
