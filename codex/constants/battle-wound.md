# Battle: Wound

Wound chance is penetration cubed against armour cubed. The cube is deliberate: it makes armour a WALL rather than a slope. A sword cut lands 976 in 1000 against a man in a shirt and 38 in 1000 against full harness — which is why armies carry hammers and mauls: high penetration crosses the wall where a sharp edge cannot.
