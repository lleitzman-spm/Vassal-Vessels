# Battle: Shock

> **STANDING — BUILT**  
> Implemented in code and checkable against the tree — a module or a test stands under it.  
> *Derived from the tree — the code is there to be read.*

When something moving hits something standing, the damage depends on how many men actually collide, how heavy they are, and — this is the whole design — the SQUARE of how fast they were still going at contact. Halve the speed and you quarter the damage. Braced spears have no magic bonus against horses; they take the speed away and the square does the rest.

## Worked example

Fifty knights, full gallop, fifteen files impacting. The swing from top to bottom is nearly three hundred to one and not one line of it is a bonus table.

- **intoLooseArchersAt450:** 29.2 men killed instantly
- **intoUnbracedPikesAt377:** 14.6 men
- **intoBracedLevySpearsShakenAt382:** 10.4 men — the same braced spears, frightened and ragged
- **intoBracedLevySpearsSteadyAt125:** 1.0 men — and then the spears kill the stalled horses
- **intoBracedPikesAt40:** 0.10 men — the horses stop dead
- **orderedFromOnly25mAt180:** 4.7 men — 16 percent of a proper charge; the run-up is the weapon
- **acrossMudAt225:** 7.0 men — mud halves the speed, the square quarters the damage
