Everything else in this game is arithmetic you can check for yourself. The Spymaster decides how much of that arithmetic you are ALLOWED TO SEE. Without him you know a lord is sullen; with him you know he stands at willingness 21, that he has made common cause with his neighbour, and that his treachery is 63 — which means that if you summon him anyway, whatever happens next is your own fault. That is the whole design of this game in one seat.
