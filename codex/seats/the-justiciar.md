The Justiciar hears complaints and gives judgements, which is the only way in the game to make a great many people less angry without handing out land you can never get back. If the court is spiralling into resentment, this is the seat that stops the spiral — which is exactly why leaving it empty is so dangerous, and why the danger is invisible until it is enormous.
