# The Constable

Every castle needs men standing on it, and those men cannot march. The Constable is how you get them down: a good one takes a quarter of every garrison off the wall and puts it in your line. On the frontier that is the difference between a lord bringing you forty riders and bringing you ten — which is why appointing a Constable feels like conjuring soldiers out of nothing, and why it is the least glamorous seat that most changes the muster.
