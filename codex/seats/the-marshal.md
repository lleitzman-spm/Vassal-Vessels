The Marshal is the man who actually leads the army, because you never do: you are the crown, not a body on a horse. His real job in the game is YOUR HANDS. Order capacity becomes the battle's couriers and its standing-plan slots — with no Marshal you have two riders and four plans, with a great one six riders and eight. That is the difference between a plan and a hope, and it is the most direct thing the court does to the battle.
