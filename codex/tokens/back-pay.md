# Back Pay

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

The most direct line in the game between a decision at court and a disaster on the field. Every unpaid season adds to a contingent's treachery AND removes the morale floor that made professionals worth hiring. Men you engaged precisely because they would stand are now both the likeliest to break and the likeliest to turn — and you can read the exact number beforehand.
