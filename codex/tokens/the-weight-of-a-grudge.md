# The Weight of a Grudge

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

Never stored — always computed from the acts behind it, which is the single most important design choice in the court. Because it is a reading, making amends genuinely works: you add a record, and every future reading sees both. And because grievances add rather than merge, eleven small ones are a serious problem with no single cause to point at.
