# Days in the Cup

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

The game's title made into arithmetic. Each house is a cup holding one year of service, and the size of the cup was written into the grant when you enfeoffed them — the grant IS the contract. Because it refills whether you used it or not, unspent service is service lost, which quietly rewards a king who fights on a schedule over one who fights on impulse.
