# Experience

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

Earned by minutes of actual fighting rather than by showing up, and capped per battle so a single bloodbath does not mint an elite. It feeds resolve, which feeds the ceiling on nerve — meaning a unit that has survived three hard fights is genuinely braver than an identical one that has not. It is also the strongest argument against spending men freely: veterancy is the one thing in this game that cannot be bought, only kept alive.
