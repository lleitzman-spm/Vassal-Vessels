# The Tide

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**

> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

How the day FEELS, to men who cannot see the whole field. It is never stored anywhere: it is computed fresh from the recent event log, with older events counting for less, which means it recovers on its own if the bleeding stops. It is also the second condition on treachery — a wavering vassal turns when the Tide is against you, and not before.
