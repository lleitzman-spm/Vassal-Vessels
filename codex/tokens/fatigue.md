A one-way tap that never turns off. Fatigue slows men down, makes them miss, and eventually starts eating the ceiling on their nerve — and crucially, they ARRIVE with some, put there by the roads and the season. Heavily armoured men tire faster, which is the price of everything that makes them good, and it is why a knight who has been fighting for an hour is a different animal from one who has not.
