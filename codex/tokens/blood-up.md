# Blood Up

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

The reason won battles get thrown away. The longer a unit chases, the less interested it becomes in being called back — and since the command phase does not even run during a pursuit, you cannot call it back anyway. Cavalry that vanishes over the hill after a broken wing is the oldest mistake in warfare and this token is it.
