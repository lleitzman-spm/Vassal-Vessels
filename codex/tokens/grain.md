# Grain

> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

The clock on every campaign. Men who go without food gain hunger, hunger lowers the ceiling on their nerve, and a low ceiling loses battles before they start. Winter is dangerous not because of the cold but because forage nearly stops, so the granary drains at full rate against nothing coming in — which is how an army is destroyed without ever meeting an enemy.
