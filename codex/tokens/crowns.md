# Crowns

> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

Read from the record of every payment and receipt, never held as a balance. Coin is the one token that converts into almost every other: it buys men who owe you nothing, food that stops hunger eating your morale ceiling, amends that cool a grudge, and the freedom of your own captured vassals. Running out of it is rarely what kills you — what kills you is what you stopped paying for.
