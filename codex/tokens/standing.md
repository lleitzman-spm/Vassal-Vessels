What the realm thinks of you, and it reaches everywhere — into how much a captive of yours is worth, into whether a captain heeds an order, into whether a house sends men or excuses. Like everything else in the court it is a reading, so there is no hoarding it and no hiding a bad year: it is exactly what the record says about you, recomputed on demand.
