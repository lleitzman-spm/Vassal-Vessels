# Arrows

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

Finite, and the finiteness is the whole design. Archers who spend the battle shooting at whatever is nearest will be empty when the thing that actually matters arrives. Aimed volleys hit far harder and cost several times the arrows, which makes every shooting decision a question about what you are saving them for.
