# The Ceiling on Nerve

The most important number a player never looks at. It is the bridge from the court to the battlefield: everything you did politically arrives here, as a hard cap on how brave these men are capable of being today. Hungry men who marched a long way for a cause they do not believe in have a low ceiling, and no amount of brilliant handling will make them fight like a household guard.
