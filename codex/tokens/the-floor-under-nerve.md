# The Floor Under Nerve

> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

The counterpart to the ceiling and the sharpest lesson about hiring soldiers. A paid professional will not break below a certain point no matter how bad it gets — that steadiness is exactly what you bought. Miss a season's wages and the floor goes away, which means the men you hired precisely because they would hold are now the men most likely to leave.
