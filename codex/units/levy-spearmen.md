# Levy Spearmen

> **STANDING — BUILT**  
> Implemented in code and checkable against the tree — a module or a test stands under it.  
> *Derived from the tree — the code is there to be read.*

Farmers with a season of training, a spear and a round shield, owed by their manor for forty days a year. Their whole value is BRACING: butt in the earth, point level, and a horse will not come on. Seven metres of refusal is just barely enough to stop a gallop — and it is multiplied by how steady and how neat they are, so frightened fyrdmen with a wobbly line are ridden straight over. When the press comes at six seconds they have a bad time; a spear line wants to hit, hurt, and step back out before then.
