Dock-fighters and boarding parties off a port's ships, used to the most confined and vicious fighting there is. They matter because they carry a SWORD as well as a spear: for the first six seconds they fight at reach like spearmen, and when the press comes and the spear becomes a fence post they draw steel and keep winning. Almost nothing else on foot is good in BOTH halves of a melee — the manual's whole lesson about sidearms, standing in one unit.
