Money and bodies. A town is the second-richest thing you can hold and it raises more men than anything else in the realm — but only if you sign a Charter giving away a fifth of its taxes forever, and only twice in three years before it riots. Its crossbowmen are the same trade as a castle's, from the town guild rather than the castle guard: cheaper men, the same weapon, and the muster's arithmetic knows the difference even though the weapon sheet does not.
