Monks. They grow a great deal of food, they make your wars look holier, and they house the chapter of the Sworn Order — sixty of the best foot soldiers in the game, free, who come only if your Chaplain has blessed the war. The same blessing brings out the relic and the men who carry it. An abbey with no Chaplain to bless anything is a very good farm.
