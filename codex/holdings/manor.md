Farmland with a lord's house on it. It feeds your realm and it raises the ordinary spearmen who will make up most of every army you ever field. Call them away in autumn and the crops rot in the field: next year the manor earns you forty per cent less and raises a fifth fewer men, and its lord carries a grudge about it. That is the single most common way players hurt themselves, and the game shows you the season before you send the letter.
