# The horn is heard

> *Derived from the tree — the code is there to be read.*

The alternative to a courier: a signal that reaches everyone in range at once, instantly and cheaply, but says only one of a handful of pre-arranged things. This is why standing plans exist. Deciding before the battle what your horn will mean is how you give an order to five contingents in a second — and why an enemy who works out your horn is a real danger.
