# The Sworn Order

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**

Sixty men who will not run away from anything, ever, for nothing. The catch is that they are certain they know God's will better than you do, so at some point in a battle they will do what they think is right instead of what you asked. Resolve 95 and obedience 55 is a very particular kind of nightmare to command.
