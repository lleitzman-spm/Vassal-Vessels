Cavalry in a blunt arrowhead: half the frontage, so half as many men can fight — but the shock of the charge is concentrated into the point and multiplied by 1.7. A wedge is a chisel: it punches a hole in one specific place. Once the charge is spent, the same narrow front means very few of your riders can actually swing at anybody — and if the point stops on braced points, the whole wedge piles up behind it.
