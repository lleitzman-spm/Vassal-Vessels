Four men wide and long as a road — the shape armies move in, never the shape they fight in. On a road it is half again as fast. It is also helpless: only four men can fight, and a column caught in contact has no front at all — everything lands as a flank. Reinforcements arrive in column, and buying them the eight-plus seconds to shake out of it is your problem.
