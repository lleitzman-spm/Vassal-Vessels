The one shallow place in the river. Slow, exhausting, in column, at four-tenths cohesion — a unit coming out of a ford is not a unit, it is a queue. Whoever holds a ford fights the enemy a few files at a time however many thousands are waiting behind. Anybody who lets you finish crossing has made a mistake; anybody who hits you halfway across has won.
