# Woodland

Trees do four things at once and every one matters. Cohesion is capped at half, so no formation holds together. Close order is forbidden outright — no shield wall, no pike hedge, no wedge. Cover cuts arrow hits to well under half. And woods BLOCK LINE OF SIGHT, so there is no signal from your banner: every order into a wood is a courier ride. Cavalry in woodland is worth almost nothing. Archers in woodland are worth almost nothing. Light foot in woodland is worth everything.
