The best-fed season and the worst political one. There is food everywhere for an army to forage — and every man you call away is a man not bringing the crops in. A manor summoned at harvest earns you forty per cent less next year, raises a fifth fewer soldiers, and its lord carries a grudge. This is the trap that catches everybody once.
