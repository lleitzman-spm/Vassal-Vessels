> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

Run away on purpose so the enemy chases you out of his own line, then turn and fight him strung out and disordered. When it works it is the most beautiful thing in the game. What makes it a real gamble rather than a clever trick is that the simulation does not distinguish a false rout from a true one where FEAR is concerned — and neither can your neighbours. They see men running; they lose heart. If the enemy does not bite, you have started your own collapse.
