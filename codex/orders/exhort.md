# Exhort

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

The commander rides down the line and shouts at them. A great surge of heart at once, plus fifteen seconds of better obedience — which is often exactly what turns a refusal into an obedience at the moment it matters. You get two, plus one more for every forty points of the commander's standing at court, so this is a court number spending itself on the field. Requires line of sight from the Crown Banner: you cannot shout at men you cannot see.
