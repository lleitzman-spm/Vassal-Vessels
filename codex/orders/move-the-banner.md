Move the Crown Banner. Wherever it stands, units that can see it take your orders in one second instead of nine, and every friend within a hundred metres is steadied. So the banner's position is your voice's position, and moving it is a strategic act. Put it forward and you can command the fighting line directly. Put it forward and it can be reached — and if it falls, every unit in the army loses a quarter of its will at once and every order you ever give afterwards is a courier ride.
