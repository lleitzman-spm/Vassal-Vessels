# Seal a Standing Plan

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

A sealed order with a condition written on the outside: 'if horsemen come within a hundred paces, brace'. The captain weighs it when it ARRIVES — he can refuse to take the envelope — but once he has taken it, the order fires INSTANTLY when the condition is met, with no rider, no delay and no second thoughts, because he agreed to it while he was calm. This is how you are in three places at once in a game that never pauses, and it is the loophole in the politics: you cannot make a disloyal lord obey you in a crisis, but you can get him to agree to something hypothetical at the morning briefing. See data/standing-plans.json.
