Butt of the spear in the earth, point level, shields locked. THE defence against cavalry — and it is not a bonus. It is a refusal field, twelve metres deep for pikes and seven for spears, that takes the speed out of a charge before it arrives; and because shock is the SQUARE of the speed left at contact, taking most of the speed away takes almost all of the damage away. Two traps. It is FRONT ARC ONLY, so a braced block hit from the side has no refusal at all. And refusal is multiplied by your cohesion and your morale, so a shaken, ragged brace is mostly decorative.
