# Sound the Retreat

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

End the battle now, as a withdrawal rather than a rout. Units disengage in good order if they can; men already routing are simply lost. This is a legitimate and often correct choice, because the court cares far less about the field than about the ARMY. A lord who walks away with eight men in ten can fight again in three weeks. A lord who is 'still winning' with four in ten cannot. Knowing the difference is most of lordship.
