# Open Ranks

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

Spread out. Arrows care about one thing above all others: how tightly packed you are. Close order takes three and a half times the arrows loose order does — and that is not a resistance stat, it is just space. The price is that a loose formation cannot push, cannot hold and cannot receive a charge, which is exactly what the enemy's cavalry commander is waiting for.
