Step backwards facing the enemy, in order, not running. One of the three most important orders in the game, because it is how spearmen escape the press before the press kills them, and how you save a wing that is losing. It is also the order most likely to be refused, because you are asking frightened men in contact to do something delicate. Whether you CAN give it was decided at court, months ago, when you decided how these men felt about you.
