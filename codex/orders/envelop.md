# Envelop

> **STANDING — PROPOSED ⚠**  
> NOT BUILT. A design in `data/` with no engine reading it yet. This page may NEVER be cited as evidence that the game plays this way.  
> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

Send a contingent wide around the enemy's flank and into his side or back. The payoff is enormous: a flank attack is worth about four times a frontal one and a rear attack about eight, because the shields point the wrong way, fewer men can swing back, and the fear is crushing. The cost is time, and time is what the enemy is also spending. Half the battles you lose with this order, you lose in the centre while you were being clever on the right.
