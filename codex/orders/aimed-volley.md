> *NOT backed by anything in the tree yet; this is the compiler's default for a design in `data/` with no engine behind it.*

Every archer draws together and looses at one named target on a count. Sixty per cent more hits for twice the arrows. Because arrows FLY — three seconds to reach a hundred and fifty metres — you are shooting at where a unit is going to be, and against something that is moving you will usually miss. Aimed volleys are for stationary, dense, committed targets: a pike block, a shield wall that has just braced, cavalry stalled on stakes.
